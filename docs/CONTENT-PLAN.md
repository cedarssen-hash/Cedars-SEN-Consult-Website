# Website Content Plan

## Writing principles
Use clear, direct, parent-friendly language. Explain professional terms. Avoid jargon, stigma, pity-based language, and exaggerated claims. Do not guarantee outcomes. Do not invent credentials, clinical protocols, diagnostic scope, testimonials, partnerships, statistics, or event details. Mark unconfirmed information as placeholder and list it for confirmation.

## Homepage hero
Headline: “Every Child Is Unique. Every Child Deserves the Opportunity to Thrive.”
Suggested copy: “Cedars Special Needs Consult provides professional assessment, behavioural and developmental support, educational services and practical guidance for children, families, schools and healthcare institutions.”
Primary CTA: Book a Consultation. Secondary CTA: Explore Our Services.

## Service pages
Create dedicated content for all eight service areas listed in the project brief. Explain what the service is, who it may support, what it may involve, Cedars' confirmed approach, FAQs, related services, and how to enquire.

## About
Include organisation profile, approach, values, mission, vision, and team information only when approved/verified. Mission and vision in the project brief are drafts, not official statements.

## Audience content
Families: explain support, how to enquire, and what information is useful for an initial conversation. Do not request confidential medical records via the public form.
Schools: explain SEN support, teacher training, behavioural support, consultancy, and SEN facility setup.
Hospitals/healthcare: explain relevant services and potential consultation/collaboration pathways without implying existing partnerships.

## Programmes
The Parent's Playbook: reusable programme page with description, topic, date/time, speakers, registration link, and past-edition area. Confirm event details before publication.
Quarterly Outreach: explain awareness and free consultation purpose. Confirm dates, eligibility, location, registration, and availability.

## FAQs
Suggested questions: What does Cedars do? Who can access services? When might a child benefit from assessment? Assessment vs therapy? Does Cedars work with schools/hospitals? What is PMT? How can a school enquire about SEN consultancy? How do I book? Where is Cedars located? Answers must reflect confirmed practice.

## Testimonials and team
Only publish authentic, approved testimonials and verified biographies. Obtain appropriate permission for names, quotations, images, and child-related information.

## Resources
Categories: Child Development; Behaviour and Parenting; Autism and Neurodevelopment; Communication and Language; Learning Disabilities; Special Education; Inclusive Schools; Parent Education. Do not fabricate articles, references, or expert authorship.
