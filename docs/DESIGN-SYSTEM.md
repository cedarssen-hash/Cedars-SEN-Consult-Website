# Design System — Cedars Special Needs Consult

## Brand reference
The supplied Cedars logo is the primary visual reference. It features a deep-green circular emblem and green wordmark on white. Add the original logo asset under `public/images/logo/` when it is available to the repository. Until then, use a clearly marked placeholder; do not recreate it as a final asset.

## Colour direction
- Primary: deep forest green sampled from the supplied logo.
- Secondary: muted sage green.
- Supporting: light green.
- Background: white and very light warm neutrals.
- Text: deep charcoal/near-black.
- Borders: accessible neutral grey or restrained green tint.
Do not use blue as the primary brand colour. During Phase 0, sample exact logo colours if possible; otherwise propose close hex values and request approval before implementation.

## Typography
Suggested headings: Poppins. Suggested body: Inter. Use readable sizes, comfortable line-height, clear hierarchy, and restrained weights.

## Visual style
Professional, contemporary, welcoming, parent-centred, trustworthy, inclusive, clean, and uncluttered. Use white-dominant layouts, forest-green CTAs, restrained sage backgrounds, 12–16px card radii, subtle shadows, consistent spacing, simple icons, and clear hierarchy. Avoid childish/cartoon styling, excessive brightness, excessive animation, crowded layouts, and generic unrelated corporate photography.

## Photography and representation
Prioritise respectful imagery of Black people, particularly African children, parents, caregivers, teachers, and professionals. Relevant scenes: family interaction, learning, therapy, consultation, inclusive classrooms, parent training, and school/healthcare settings. Images must be dignified and non-stereotypical; never frame disability as tragedy or use pity-based imagery.

Pinterest is for visual inspiration only, not permission to republish. Use Cedars-approved photographs, licensed stock, or images with documented permission. Do not imply stock subjects are actual Cedars clients or staff. Use placeholders when suitable imagery is unavailable. Provide meaningful alt text; decorative images should have empty alt text.

## Accessibility and responsive design
Target WCAG 2.2 AA at minimum. Use semantic HTML, logical heading hierarchy, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, labelled forms, useful errors, reduced-motion support, responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts, and ensure the WhatsApp control does not obscure mobile content.

## Reusable components
Header/mobile navigation, footer, buttons/links, section containers/headings, service cards, audience cards, programme cards, FAQs, contact CTAs, form fields/validation, and breadcrumbs where useful.

## Approval
Do not lock final colour hex values until the logo has been inspected and the proposed palette presented for approval.
