# Project Brief — Cedars Special Needs Consult Website

## Project overview
Build a professional, responsive, accessible, search-friendly public website for Cedars Special Needs Consult. The site should communicate trust, inclusion, professional support, and practical guidance for children, families, schools, and healthcare institutions.

This is a public information and enquiry website. It is separate from the professional Neurodevelopmental Practice and Care Management SaaS application. Do not implement clinical records, child case files, confidential assessment storage, therapy-session records, or clinical workflow management here.

## Organisation profile
Cedars Special Needs Consult supports children and persons with developmental differences, learning disabilities, and behavioural challenges. Cedars works with families, children's hospitals, and K–12 schools.

## Services
1. Behavioural assessment and behavioural therapy.
2. Communication and language therapy.
3. Assessment services.
4. Parent Management Training (PMT).
5. Special Educational Needs (SEN) support, including remedial education, bridge courses, individualised educational support, IEPs, goals, and milestones.
6. Teacher training.
7. Consultation.
8. School consultancy and SEN facility setup.

Do not invent clinical instruments, treatment protocols, diagnostic scope, practitioner credentials, or guaranteed outcomes. Request confirmation where details are missing.

## Audiences
- Parents and families: service explanations, how to begin, assessment/support options, contact routes.
- K–12 schools: SEN support, teacher training, behavioural support, consultancy, facility setup, individualised educational support.
- Children's hospitals and healthcare institutions: behavioural assessment, therapy, parent management training, professional consultation, and potential collaboration. Do not claim existing partnerships unless verified.
