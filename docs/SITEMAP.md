# Website Sitemap and Page Structure

## Primary navigation
Home · About Us · Services · Who We Work With · Programmes · Resources · Contact Us

Header calls to action: **Book a Consultation** and **Chat on WhatsApp**.

## Home page
1. Header/navigation.
2. Hero headline: “Every Child Is Unique. Every Child Deserves the Opportunity to Thrive.”
3. Trust/service introduction: family-centred support, school partnerships, healthcare collaboration. Do not invent statistics or claims.
4. Services cards for all eight service areas.
5. About Cedars introduction.
6. Who We Work With: families, K–12 schools, children's hospitals/healthcare institutions.
7. How support may work: Reach Out → Initial Consultation → Assessment and Planning (where appropriate) → Intervention and Support → Review/Ongoing Guidance (where appropriate).
8. Programmes: The Parent's Playbook and Quarterly Outreach.
9. Testimonials only when genuine and approved; otherwise omit.
10. FAQs.
11. Contact CTA.
12. Footer.

## About Us
Organisation profile, approach, values, mission/vision (draft wording requires approval), and verified team profiles only when supplied.

## Services overview and detail pages
Create an overview page and dedicated pages for:
- Behavioural Assessment and Behavioural Therapy.
- Communication and Language Therapy.
- Assessment Services.
- Parent Management Training.
- Special Educational Needs Support.
- Teacher Training.
- Consultation.
- School Consultancy and SEN Facility Setup.

Each detail page should include service-specific introduction, who it may support, needs it may address, Cedars' confirmed approach, what it may involve, FAQs, related services, and enquiry CTA.

## Who We Work With
- Parents and Families.
- Schools (K–12).
- Children's Hospitals and Healthcare Institutions.
Give each audience tailored information and enquiry CTA.

## Programmes
- Programmes overview.
- The Parent's Playbook.
- Quarterly Outreach.
Support upcoming events, topics, speakers, registration links, and past editions. Use placeholders until confirmed.

## Resources
Resource landing page and article template. Suggested categories: Child Development; Behaviour and Parenting; Autism and Neurodevelopment; Communication and Language; Learning Disabilities; Special Education; Inclusive Schools; Parent Education. Do not fabricate articles or research claims. CMS is optional future scope and needs approval.

## Contact Us
Contact details, general enquiry form, institutional enquiry route/form, WhatsApp CTA. Show map only after address verification.

## FAQs
Questions may cover services, eligibility, assessment, therapy, school/hospital work, PMT, booking, and location. Answers must be based on confirmed facts.

## Privacy Policy
Privacy information and consent practices; legal/privacy content requires appropriate review.

## Footer
Logo and short description; navigation; service links; contact details; supplied social links only; Privacy Policy; copyright notice.
